🥗 **Welcome to Food Share – Connecting Food with Community!**

This app is a solution for reducing food waste by easily connecting individuals and organizations with surplus food to those in need within their local community. It's built on a foundation of sustainability and local goodwill.

Key Features

📍
Local Discovery

Find listings for free food available nearby.

🤝
Easy Posting

Upload surplus food items in minutes with a photo.

💬
In-App Chat

Secure communication between donor and recipient.

✅
Verified Users

A safe community focus for food handling.
